01 About me

I started in the Army as a computer and detection systems repairer, the soldier who fixed the equipment other people trusted. Through the Green to Gold program I earned a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics, commissioned as an officer, and went on to teach the Army's Cyber Captains as an instructor and curriculum developer at the U.S. Army Cyber School.

Today I'm a strategic defensive cyberspace operations planner and knowledge management officer, where I also design and teach AI fundamentals to senior joint staff officers. I'm finishing an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance (WGU, Dec 2026) and an M.B.A. in IT Management (WGU, 2027).

What drives me: making hard technical ideas usable, whether that's a classroom of 60 officers or an AI agent that explains a security alert to a non-technical manager.
