01 · ABOUT ME

Build understanding.
Help people grow.

I'm a Cyber Operations Planner with a background in electronics repair, communications, cybersecurity and instruction. I started as an Army Small Electronics Repairman, earned a B.S. in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics, worked on an offensive cyber team, and taught at the Cyber School.

What ties it together: I like making difficult ideas easier to understand, and then proving them by building something that works. Lately that means AI agents on AWS that explain security to the people who have to act on it.

My wife and I have four children. I'm finishing an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an M.B.A. in IT Management.
