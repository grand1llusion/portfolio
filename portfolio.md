## Creative feature: the Secure Terminal
A visible ">_" button and hint open a terminal over the page. Visitors type commands (whoami, ls projects,
cat superpowers, traceroute career) and hunt for three hidden flags; finding all three unlocks a
"you'd pass my course" certificate. Esc or `exit` closes it; the rest of the site works without it.
