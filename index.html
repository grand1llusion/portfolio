01 About me

I'm a cybersecurity professional who likes the whole path from packets to decisions: capturing and reading traffic, hunting through logs, tuning detections, and then turning what I find into something a non-specialist can act on.

Lately that means building AI agents on AWS: an incident responder whose actions are checked by a decision model, a triage agent wired to live Amazon GuardDuty findings, and a Scrum team simulator built with Strands Agents. I'm finishing an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an M.B.A. in IT Management.

What drives me: making hard technical ideas usable, and proving skills by building things you can run, not just listing them.
