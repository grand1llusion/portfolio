# Portfolio plan: Bradley Papke

The site is built from this plan and from `data.js` (the timeline).

## Story
"Still asking how things work." A hand-me-down computer in a childhood bedroom starts a journey through
electronics repair, cyber operations and teaching. One illustrated desk scene per year, 2001 to 2026, with 2–3
callouts on real objects in each picture. Source: Timeline_Data.xlsx (cleaned copy), reviewed Nov 6, 2026.

## Sections
- Journey: the time-travelling desk (slider, year buttons, callouts, family photo, what's next)
- About me: Cyber Operations Planner, electronics → cyber → teaching → AI agents; family
- Tech superpowers: six cards
- Projects and achievements: AntiSOCial, SOC It To 'Em, Sprint Zero, this portfolio; certifications by year;
  CTF wins; education
- Contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub. No phone number. Footer: "Views are my own."

## Creative feature
Every screen in the scenes is a real terminal. Every year is a directory: cd, ls, cat <object>, history,
traceroute career. Three flags (traceroute career, cat .logs, sudo hire-me) unlock a certificate.
